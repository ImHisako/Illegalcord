# Illegalcord Code of Conduct

Illegalcord welcomes users and contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat others with respect and help keep the community open to questions, experimentation, and constructive discussion.

## Open discussion

You are welcome to question project decisions, criticize features, suggest alternatives, and discuss other Discord clients by name. Disagreement with maintainers, preference for another client, or using AI as a development tool is not, by itself, a reason for moderation. Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback, and explain concerns with concrete examples.
- Help newcomers and respect different levels of technical experience.
- Respect personal boundaries and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Protect other people's privacy when sharing screenshots, logs, or reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, or targeted personal attacks.
- Sharing private information, credentials, or private communications without permission.
- Spam, impersonation, or deliberate disruption of discussions and collaboration.
- Encouraging others to harass someone or retaliating against someone for reporting a concern.

## Scope and moderation

These expectations apply to Illegalcord's repository, issues, pull requests, and official community spaces. They support the project's freedom of discussion while protecting people's ability to participate.

Maintainers and moderators assess the behavior and context of each incident. Depending on its severity and whether it is repeated, they may request a change in behavior, remove content, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Serious abuse may result in immediate action.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns to an Illegalcord maintainer or a moderator of the affected community space. For sensitive reports, use a private contact method they provide. Include relevant links and context, and avoid posting personal information or credentials publicly. Do not organize retaliation or public harassment.

If you believe a moderation decision was mistaken, contact the maintainer or moderator who handled it and explain the relevant context respectfully.

Official project links are available on the [Illegalcord website](https://illegalcord.netlify.app/). Project news and updates are published on [Illegalcord's Telegram channel](https://t.me/Illegalcord).
